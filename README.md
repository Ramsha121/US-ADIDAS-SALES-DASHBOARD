📊 Adidas US Sales Dashboard Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Adidas US sales data using data visualization techniques to uncover key business insights. The dashboard highlights sales performance across products, regions, retailers, and sales channels, helping in data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance and trends
Identify top-performing product categories
Understand seasonal sales patterns
Evaluate regional and retailer contributions
Discover opportunities for business growth

📈 Key Insights
1. Strong Overall Sales Performance
Total Sales: $900M+
Total Transactions: 825K
Units Sold: 2M+
Indicates strong brand presence and high market demand

3. Top Performing Category
Men’s Athletic Footwear generated the highest revenue (~$82.8M)
Core strength lies in performance-focused men’s products

4. Seasonal Sales Trends
Sales dip in February–March
Peak observed in July–August (~$100M)
Recovery in December due to holiday demand

5. Retailer Performance
West Gear leads in both sales and profit
Followed by Foot Locker and Sports Direct
Lower contribution from Kohl’s and Amazon

6. Regional Analysis
West region dominates sales
Followed by Northeast and Southeast
Midwest shows lowest performance

7. Sales Channel Contribution
In-store: 39.6% (Highest)
Outlet: 32.9%
Online: 27.5%
Physical retail remains a key driver

8. Growth Opportunity
Revenue is concentrated in men’s products
Potential to expand women’s product segment

💡 Business Recommendations
Invest more in men’s athletic footwear category
Strengthen online sales channels
Improve performance in Midwest region
Optimize partnerships with underperforming retailers
Expand women’s product line for future growth


🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Power BI / Tableau (Dashboard Visualization)
Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)
Data Analysis Techniques
